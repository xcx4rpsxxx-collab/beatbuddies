Beat Buddies Support

Need help or found a problem? Open a support request. Requests are public, so please leave out personal details.

How do I play?

Tap a buddy to add it to the stage and the music starts. Every buddy plays its own part, and they all stay in time. Tap a buddy on the stage to mute it, or use its slider to change its volume.

Do I need internet or an account?

No. Everything is in the app, and there is nothing to sign in to.
